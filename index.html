Allah tumhein lambi, sehatmand aur nek zindagi ata farmaye. Tumhari zindagi ko bepanah khushiyon, rehmat aur har kaam mein barkat se bhar de. Tumhara dil hamesha sukoon mein rahe aur tum hamesha hansti, muskurati raho.

Allah tumhein har buri nazar, har takleef aur har pareshani se mehfooz rakhe. Tumhein imaan ki roshni, dil ka sukoon aur nek naseeb ata farmaye. Tum hamesha apne Ammi-Abbu aur poori family ki aankhon ka noor bani raho.

Allah tumhare dil mein Huzoor Nabi-e-Kareem ﷺ ki bepanah mohabbat paida farmaye. Allah tumhari zindagi ko deen ke noor se roshan kare aur tumhein hamesha seedhe raste par chalaye.

Allah tumhare Ammi-Abbu ko sehat, sukoon, lambi umr aur khushiyan ata farmaye. Unki har pareshani door kare, unki mehnaton mein barkat de aur unhein apni aulaad ki khushiyan dekhna naseeb farmaye. Allah tumhare poore ghar ko mohabbat, ittehad, rehmat aur barkat se bhar de. Tumhari family ko har museebat aur buri nazar se mehfooz rakhe aur ghar mein hamesha sukoon aur khushhaali qayam rakhe. Aur haa tumhari bhi bure logon se hifazat kare

Allah tumko aur sabko duniya aur aakhirat dono mein kamyabi ata farmaye, Huzoor ﷺ ki mohabbat aur Sunnat par chalne ki taufeeq de aur apni khaas rehmat se nawaze.
